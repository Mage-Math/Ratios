💡 How Ratio Parts Work:
A ratio of 5 : 2 means for every 5 parts of Sultanas, there are 2 parts of Cherries.
• Step 1: Find the value of 1 single part by dividing the total weight of Cherries ($80\text{g}$) by its $2$ parts.
• Step 2: Multiply that 1-part value by $5$ to find the weight of Sultanas!
